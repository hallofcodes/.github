## Build together.

Hall of Codes brings developers, maintainers, and curious builders together to create meaningful projects, share knowledge, and help one another grow.

Started in 2019, we believe great software comes from people who are willing to collaborate, learn from different perspectives, and turn ideas into something real.

**Build together. Argue less. Ship more.**

## What we're about

- **Open source:** Build and maintain useful projects that others can learn from and contribute to.
- **Collaboration:** Connect developers across experience levels, backgrounds, and time zones.
- **Mentorship:** Help newcomers find their footing and give contributors room to grow.
- **Knowledge sharing:** Exchange ideas through discussions, workshops, tutorials, and community events.
- **Real-world impact:** Turn individual skills into projects that make a difference.

## Join the community

You don't need to know everything to get started. Bring your curiosity, share what you're building, ask questions, or find people who can help bring your next idea to life.

## Featured projects

### [Rutex: AI Agent Plugin for Acode](https://github.com/hallofcodes/acode-ai-agent-plugin)

An autonomous AI coding assistant for the Acode editor on Android. It enables compatible language models to inspect code, create files, refactor projects, and execute terminal commands through an agentic workflow.

`TypeScript` `AI` `Android` `Developer Tools`

### [WarpShare](https://github.com/hallofcodes/WarpShare)

A cross-platform file-sharing CLI for transferring files over local networks and remote connections, designed to make sharing straightforward without unnecessary cloud intermediaries.

`Networking` `P2P` `CLI` `Cross-platform`

### [DevPulse](https://github.com/hallofcodes/devpulse)

A developer productivity platform bringing coding activity, project progress, and team insights together. Explore activity dashboards, leaderboards, and tools for managing collaborative work.

`Productivity` `Analytics` `Dashboard` `Developer Tools`

### [WISP](https://github.com/hallofcodes/wisp)

A public-health data platform focused on making infectious disease information easier to discover, monitor, and understand through aggregated surveillance data and geographic insights.

`Public Health` `Data Analytics` `TypeScript` `Geospatial`
